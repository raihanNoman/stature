# Understanding Your Stature

**Author:** Raihan Noman  
**Date:** April 19, 2026

Stature is more than just a score; it is a global measure of your economic integrity and reliability across the entire blockchain ecosystem. Unlike traditional credit scores that are hidden behind black boxes, the **Stature.network** protocol is transparent, mathematical, and earned through your actions.

---

## The Core Philosophy
Stature is built on three unbreakable pillars:
1. **Real Value:** Every point is backed by a verifiable transaction. We do not track "social likes."
2. **Consistency:** Trust is built over time. A steady "heartbeat" of activity is worth more than a one-time splash.
3. **Domain Integrity:** High-stakes actions (like insurance or legal contracts) carry more weight than micro-tasks.

---

## How the Math Works
Your Stature gain for any given action is calculated using the following protocol:

$$S = \sum (V_log 	imes D_w 	imes T_c)$$

### 1. Economic Magnitude ($V_log$)
We use a **Logarithmic Scale** to measure the value of your transaction.
* **Why?** This ensures that while larger transactions earn more stature, they don't completely eclipse smaller users. It prevents "Whales" from buying their way to a perfect reputation instantly.
* **Effect:** A $1,000 transaction is worth more than a $10 one, but not 100x more.

### 2. Domain Weight ($D_w$)
Each program you interact with has a weight assigned by the Stature Admins based on the industry's "Trust Requirement."
* **Education/Micro-tasks:** Lower weights (e.g., 1.0x - 2.0x).
* **Insurance/Finance:** Medium weights (e.g., 5.0x).
* **Legal/Contracts:** High weights (e.g., 8.0x+).

### 3. The Pulse Multiplier ($T_c$)
This is your **Activity Density**. It measures how consistent you are across the entire network.
* **High Pulse:** A user who interacts once a day has a high density and receives a **bonus multiplier** (up to 2.0x) on all points earned.
* **Low Pulse:** A user who interacts once a year receives no bonus. Consistency is the fastest way to level up.

## The Leveling Journey
Your total Stature points determine your **Stature Level (1-100)**. We use a quadratic curve ($100 \times Level^2$), meaning every new level is harder to reach than the last.

| Level Range | Status | Benefits |

## What Can Lower Your Stature?
Stature is a living score. Just as you can earn "Honor Points," you can also receive **"Slashing Penalties."**
* **Defaults:** Failing to honor a contract or insurance payment.
* **Malicious Behavior:** Programs can report negative values for broken agreements.
* **The Result:** A negative transaction subtracts stature from your total, which could result in a **Level Demotion**.
