### Welcome to the Stature.network Ecosystem
As a **Registered Program Operator**, you are a "Validator of Integrity." Your integration with the Stature protocol directly impacts the global reputation and financial mobility of your users. 

This document outlines the mechanical logic, scope, and responsibilities associated with the `update_user_stature_via_cpi` function.

## 1. Protocol Scope & Philosophy
Stature.network is an **Economic Reputation Layer**. 
* **Value-Driven:** Reputation is derived from verifiable economic exchanges (Lamports/Tokens).
* **Anti-Social:** We do not support "Social Points" or "Likes." Every point of Stature must represent a real-world risk or contribution.
* **Bi-Directional:** Stature is not "Up-Only." It is a living score that can increase with honor or decrease with default.

---

## 2. The Mathematics of Trust
When you call `update_user_stature_via_cpi`, the protocol passes the `raw_value` (transaction amount) through a three-pillar algorithm:

### A. Economic Magnitude ($V_log$)
We use a **Logarithmic Scale** (Binary Log via leading zeros) to process transaction values.
* **Why:** This prevents "Whales" from buying their way to the top. A $1,000,000 transaction is worth more than $1,000, but it is not 1,000x more valuable in terms of reputation.
* **Operator Tip:** If your program deals with low-liquidity (e.g., Education/Social Work), we compensate for low $V_log$ by assigning your program a higher **Domain Weight**.

### B. The Pulse Multiplier ($T_c$)
We measure the **Activity Density** of the user across the entire Stature ecosystem.
* **Formula:** $Total\ Records / Months\ Since\ First\ Action$.
* **Impact:** A "Hustler" who interacts consistently every day earns trust faster than a "Ghost" who makes one large transaction and disappears for a year.

### C. Domain Weight ($D_w$)
The multiplier assigned to your program during registration. 
* **Low Risk (e.g., Micro-payments):** $1.0x - 2.0x$
* **High Integrity (e.g., Legal/Insurance):** $5.0x - 10.0x$

---

## 3. Function Logic & Parameters

### `raw_value` (i64)
* **Positive Values:** Represents successful payments, contributions, or honored agreements.
* **Negative Values (Slashing):** Represents defaults, broken contracts, or malicious behavior. 
* **Requirement:** This should be the raw amount in **Lamports** or the smallest unit of your SPL token.

### `memo` (String)
* You must provide a clear, concise reason for the update (e.g., "Monthly Premium Paid", "Contract #282 Signed", "Loan Default Penalty"). 
* This is stored on-chain in the `StatureRecord` and serves as the user's permanent audit trail.

---

## 4. Operator Responsibilities

### A. Integrity of Reporting
You are the "Source of Truth" for the Stature protocol. Reporting "fake" transactions to pump a user's score is a violation of the network's integrity. Stature.network reserves the right to:
1. **Lower Domain Weights:** If a program is found to have low-quality data.
2. **Suspend Program:** If malicious "Stature Farming" is detected.

### B. User Transparency
Users have a right to know why their Stature changed. Ensure your app UI reflects the `raw_value` and `memo` you send to the protocol.

### C. Slashing (Negative Updates)
Operators are encouraged to use negative `raw_value` updates for defaults. This protects other programs in the ecosystem from high-risk users. **Use this power judiciously.**

---

## 5. Technical Limitations
* **Overflow Protection:** All math is checked. If a transaction or total exceeds `i64::MAX`, the transaction will fail with an `Overflow` error.
* **CPI Authority:** Only the specific Program PDA authorized during registration can call the update function for that program ID.

### Summary of our Protocol Architecture

* **Strictly Economic:** Stature is a financial reputation protocol. No "social likes." Every point is backed by a verifiable $i64$ transaction value.
* **Bi-Directional Honor:** Registered programs have the power to reward ($+$) or slash ($-$) a user's reputation based on real-world performance.
* **Global Identity:** A user's "Pulse" (activity density) follows them across all programs, making the ecosystem "sticky" and rewarding consistent "Citizens."
